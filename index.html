Your Family as Collectible Cards

Made to get young ones curious about their roots, so every ancestor becomes a card worth collecting.

🔒 Everything runs in this browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

If your browser asks to let this site view files, that only lets this page read them on your device.

How it works

  1. 1

    Export

    Save your tree as a .ged file from any genealogy app.

    Everything on a card comes from this file. KinDex shows it as cards; it never adds or changes anything about your family.

  2. 2

    Add photos

    Make one folder with your .ged file and a photos folder. Name each photo after the person:

    Hartwell Family/
├── hartwell-family.ged
└── photos/
    ├── Arthur Hartwell.jpg
    ├── Thomas Hartwell.png
    └── Daniel Kim.jpg

    Capitals and spaces don't matter. Two people with the same name? Add the birth year: Maya Hartwell 1983.jpg

  3. 3

    Open

    Click Open family folder, or drag the folder onto this page.

    No photos? Click Open a .ged file and pick just the .ged file. Cards without a photo show initials.

  4. 4

    Share

    Click Download to share to save one file with every card and photo.

    Send it to relatives. They just open it, no setup needed.

Privacy

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your GEDCOM file and photos are read by your browser, on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Nothing is kept. No accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking. Closing the tab clears your tree.
  • Downloads stay local. “Download to share” and “Save image” create the file on your device and save it straight to your Downloads folder.
  • Standard hosting logs. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep basic server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) to deliver this page.
